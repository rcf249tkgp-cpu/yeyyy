Why VYRO

Nobody claps at 5:40 AM. You train anyway.

VYRO started in a garage gym with a squat rack, a bad heater and a rule: you don't miss Mondays. We couldn't find training gear that looked as serious as the people wearing it, so we made our own.

Every piece is heavyweight, cut for a lifter's frame and tested under a bar before it ships. No slogans across the chest. No shortcuts in the fabric. You earn the pump. The gear just has to keep up.
